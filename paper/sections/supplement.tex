\subsection*{S1 Text. Pre-registered predictions and their scoring}

\paragraph{Dose-response (registered before any fit)} P1: within each arm and
regime, the excess basal error (hybrid minus control) is monotone in achieved
anchor ratio (Spearman $\ge 0.8$). P2: the retinoid arm falls below 2\% at $k=0$
and below 3\% by $k=0.03$. P3: the single-knockdown arm never falls below 2\% at
any dose (the registered falsifier: a collapse would mean knockdown depth, not
anchor visitation, is doing the work). P4: the double-knockdown arm at $k=0$ is
below 2\% and below the single-knockdown arm at $k=0$. P5: the retinoid and
double-knockdown arms lie on one curve against ratio. P6: matched ratios reached
by different means give errors within a factor of about 2. Scoring with the best
available data (Normal two restarts, Severe five): P1 fails (four of six
arm--regime cells pass at $\rho_s=0.94$--$1.00$; \texttt{wnt}/Normal $+0.58$ and
\texttt{ra}/Severe $+0.26$ fail); P2 passes in Normal (0.0\%, 0.2\%) and fails in
Severe (3.3\% against a mechanistic-control floor of 3.4\%); P3 fails in Normal
(1.3\%) and passes in Severe (2.5\%); P4, P5 and P6 pass in both regimes (P5 gap
0.4 and 3.3 points; P6 $\times2.0$, $\times2.3$). Three of six pass in both
regimes. With two rather than five restarts in Severe, P3 and P5 also failed
there: P3 because the single knockdown reached 1.5\% at $k=0$ (2.6\% with five
restarts), P5 because of one bad optimum at $k=0.1$ (23.4\% between neighbours
at 0.8\% and 3.3\%), which five restarts removed.

\paragraph{Prospective test (registered before any fit)} Q1: \texttt{m\_h13}
basal error below 3\% under the prescribed protocol (\texttt{mycKO}). Q2: under
the near-miss \texttt{bcatKO} it stays above 8\% in at least one regime (the
falsifier). Q3: the equation-parameter count rises under \texttt{mycKO} and not
under \texttt{bcatKO}. Q4: the functional error under \texttt{mycKO} falls below
20\% in Normal. Q5 (secondary, flagged in advance as confounded): \texttt{h13\_b}
basal error below 5\% under \texttt{hox13KO}. Scoring: Q1 passes in both regimes;
Q2 passes; Q3 passes in Normal and fails in Severe; Q4 fails (68.4\%); Q5 fails
in both regimes. Two of five pass.

\subsection*{S2 Text. Training and computational details}

\paragraph{Inverse PINN} One state network per condition (Methods); 150
observations per condition with Gaussian noise of standard deviation 0.002, plus
the initial condition (weight 20); 8000 collocation points; Adam ($10^{-3}$ for
the networks, $5\times10^{-3}$ for the log-parameters, 2000 epochs, cosine
schedule), then 150 L-BFGS steps and 600 parameter-refinement steps with the
networks frozen; adaptive loss weights. Parameters are initialised at
$1.5\times$ truth with $W=1.2$, $\thP=0.6$ in every regime.

\paragraph{SGN-PINN} Head features as in Eq~\eqref{eq:head}; 3000-point
residual grid; initial-condition weight $\lambda_0=100$; Levenberg--Marquardt
damping initialised at $10^{-3}$, divided by 3 on acceptance and multiplied by 5
on rejection, ridge $10^{-10}$ times the mean data-block curvature on the head
blocks; a level of the $\lambda$ schedule ends after the iteration budget, a
relative decrease below $10^{-7}$, or eight consecutive rejections. All linear
algebra in double precision on the GPU.

\paragraph{Hybrid term and screen} $2\times5$ tanh network, Glorot-uniform, final
bias zero, $L^2$ penalty $10^{-8}$ on the mean squared weight, trained jointly.
Screen: stride-8 reference grid, 3000 Adam steps at $5\times10^{-3}$, 300 L-BFGS
iterations with strong-Wolfe line search, restart seeds $100+17s$, lowest
residual kept.

\paragraph{Fisher information} Central differences with step $10^{-4}$ in
log-parameters on Radau solutions (rtol $10^{-10}$, atol $10^{-12}$), at the
150 observation times of each condition of the benchmark dataset;
$\sigma=0.002$. The design-comparison bounds for $\thP$
(Section~\ref{sec:res-thetaP}) use 200 uniformly spaced times per condition and a step of $10^{-3}$.
