# Design research — MATS poster corpus (examples-posters/, 20 photos)

Analyzed 2026-08-26. These are the user's taste reference (MATS 10.0 Research
Symposium posters, photographed in person). ~13 distinct posters, several shot
twice. What follows is what the corpus actually does, ranked by how well it
works, and the grammar to borrow.

## The standouts (emulate these)

1. **"Midtraining is noisy"** — the most Figma-like board in the set.
   Cream ground, huge left-aligned serif title, tiny mono eyebrow line above it
   (`MATS SYMPOSIUM · SUMMER 2026`), sections keyed by mono eyebrows
   (`01 METHODOLOGY`, `02 EFFECT`…), numbered step boxes with thin rules, mono
   figure captions, generous margins, almost no color beyond one accent. Proof
   that restraint + hierarchy reads as "designed" even with dense tables.
2. **"When Models Reason Silently"** — dark header band with the title and a
   *subtitle claim with color-highlighted key words*; body is white rounded
   cards on cream in a strict grid; **numbered circular badges (1–7) turn the
   whole board into a story path**; a dark "Takeaways" card bottom-right closes
   it. Densest poster in the room yet fully navigable.
3. **"How do you audit a computation you cannot see?"** — question title +
   one-sentence thesis directly under it; **every section header is a claim**
   ("Averaging shrinks the noise. It never shrinks the gap.", "The test that
   survives: subtract"); serif display; figures narrated by their captions;
   mono numerals. Dense, zero decoration, completely authoritative.
4. **"How ignorant is your unlearning method?"** — eyebrow + claim-head
   pattern (`RESULTS · OPTIMIZATION AXIS` over "Benign data restores what no
   probe could find"); an 8-card numbered pipeline (01–08) as a figure; clean
   tables with mono model names.
5. **"Is your Model Learning the Task, or the Judge?"** — full-width accent
   band: **"Only got 2 mins? Read this part!"** containing a 4-step diagram +
   "We study / We find / We claim" one-liners. The fast path made explicit.
6. **"Model organisms for selective withholding"** — same fast-path idea as a
   left sidebar card ("Only 2 minutes? Read Here"); numbered section badges.
7. **"EpistemicWorld"** — maroon full-height left sidebar (TL;DR,
   contributions, findings) — betterposter-lite; body stays traditional.

## The recurring grammar (what makes these read as designed)

- **Title is a question or a claim**, never a topic label; a one-sentence
  thesis sits directly beneath it.
- **Section headers are findings, not categories.** "Benign data restores what
  no probe could find" beats "Results". (The single strongest move in the
  corpus; the weakest posters are exactly the ones using MOTIVATION /
  EXPERIMENTS / RESULTS labels alone.)
- **An explicit 2-minute fast path**: either a full-width band under the title
  or a sidebar card, carrying a mini-diagram + we-study/we-find/we-claim.
- **Numbered story path** (circle badges or `01`-style eyebrows) ordering the
  board left→right.
- **Eyebrow + claim-head** two-level header: small caps/mono kicker naming the
  section type, serif claim underneath.
- **Cards on a cream/white ground**, hairline rules, ONE accent color family
  (MATS maroon) + neutrals; data figures carry their own restrained palette.
- **Mono type as a signal**: parameters, protocol names, numerals, captions.
- **Figures carry direct labels** and captions that state the finding, not
  "Figure 3: results".
- **A closing card**: Takeaways / Implications bottom-right, sometimes dark to
  anchor the exit; Limitations stated plainly next to it.
- Density is fine — **hierarchy, not whitespace volume, is what separates the
  good from the cluttered** in this corpus.

## Translation to our poster

- The deck's Garnet `#9C2745` ≈ MATS maroon → use Garnet as the structural
  accent (header band, eyebrows, rules, badges) over Paper `#F7F6F3`, Ink
  `#152A3A`. The professor's SA palette (blue/orange/grey/red) stays inside
  the figures — no clash, since chrome is garnet/neutral.
- Type: serif display for title + claim heads (echoes the corpus), Inter for
  body, IBM Plex Mono for eyebrows/params/numerals. (Space Grotesk already
  downloaded as an alternative display; serif matches the corpus better —
  decide in the plan.)
- Borrow: question title + thesis line, 2-minute band, numbered path, claim
  heads, dark takeaways card, honest limitations card.
