The whole project is one long fight with a single enemy: \textbf{you cannot recover the parameters of a 7-ODE WNT--RA--HOX system from a handful of noisy trajectory samples} --- it's ill-posed. Every folder is a new lever tried against that wall. Here's the story in order.

\section*{Phase 0 --- Port the model (early June)}
Started by converting the MATLAB \code{modelsys\_2025} biology model to Python/SciPy (git: ``first file converted'', ``fully converted with plot''). This gave the \textbf{7-state reduced WNT--RA--HOX ODE} \code{[b, apc, h5, h13, m, r, c]}, 4 regimes (Normal / Early adenoma / Cancer-like / Strong APC-mutant) differing only in \code{W} (WNT drive) and \code{thetaP} (APC health), and a scipy \code{solve\_ivp} Radau reference solver. This is the ground truth everything is measured against.

\section*{Phase 1 --- Forward PINN, and the ``just lines'' scare (June 20--24)}
\begin{itemize}
\item First PINN forward solve trained\ldots{} and produced \textbf{straight-line plots}. Panic: ``it didn't converge.''
\item \textbf{Diagnosis (2026-06-24): it had converged --- the flat plots were stale PNGs} from an earlier plain-MLP run. The real bug was earlier: a plain MLP on scalar time suffers \textbf{spectral bias} and physically cannot represent the circadian RA oscillation (period 24) or the ATRA pulse. $\rightarrow$ This is the \textbf{``Activation function change'' / Fourier-feature} fix in git: lift time into a bank of sines/cosines (Tancik 2020) so fast modes are representable. Re-running with Fourier features gave curvy plots sitting right on the reference (data MSE 1.4e-5).
\item \textbf{Second, deeper realization:} that ``forward PINN'' was \textbf{fake as a solver} --- it was fed \textbf{3000 dense labels} of the already-solved trajectory, so it was just supervised interpolation; the physics residual did nothing.
\item \textbf{Fix:} built \code{forward-pinn-train-hybrid/} --- the \textit{honest} forward solve: only \textbf{40 random sparse observations + IC anchor + physics residual}, so the ODE must actually fill the gaps. Also here the \textbf{tmux workflow} was established (an earlier run died mid-L-BFGS when the shell dropped).
\end{itemize}

\section*{Phase 2 --- First inverse PINN, 2 params (June 25)}
Built \code{PINN-inverse-solve/}. Flip the problem: trajectory known $\rightarrow$ \textbf{recover unknown parameters}. Started with just \code{W} and \code{thetaP}.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Result:} \code{W} recovered cleanly in \textit{every} regime; \code{thetaP} recovered only in \textbf{low-WNT} regimes (Normal 0.6\% err $\rightarrow$ Strong APC-mutant \textbf{142\% err}).
\item \textbf{Key finding (the through-line of the whole project):} this is \textbf{genuine structural non-identifiability, not a bug}. At high \code{W}, $\beta$-catenin saturates and the APC sub-dynamics go insensitive to \code{thetaP} --- the loss landscape is flat along it. First statement of the ``high-WNT \code{thetaP} wall.''
\end{itemize}

\section*{Phase 3 --- Scale to all 36 params (June 26)}
Generalized the inverse solver from 2 $\rightarrow$ \textbf{all 36 identifiable parameters} at once (derived \code{UNKNOWN} set, data-driven \code{InverseParams}, param-major reporting). Deliberately over-parameterized and ill-posed --- most params expected to recover poorly, \textit{by design}, as the input to a future sensitivity-based pruning.

\section*{Phase 4 --- ``Better'' inverse: the fixes that partly worked (June 29)}
Built \code{PINN-inverse-better/} after reading Engl et al.\ (\textit{Inverse Problems in Systems Biology}) and Peifer \& Timmer (multiple-shooting). Threw four fixes at it:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Multiple experimental conditions} (3: ctrl/noATRA/earlyATRA) --- one state-net per condition, \textbf{shared} params $\rightarrow$ the identifiability lever.
\item \textbf{Log/relative parametrization} (scale-invariant across the 0.08--3.5 span).
\item \textbf{Decoupled LR schedules} --- fixed a real \textbf{LR-freeze bug} where one cosine schedule drove the params to 1e-6 and froze them.
\item \textbf{Adaptive grad-norm loss weighting}.
\end{enumerate}
Also added the user-requested \textbf{error-vs-iteration plots} and a \textbf{per-parameter identifiability verdict} (YES/MARGINAL/NO + csv/json).
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Result: disappointing --- only 6/36 under 10\%,} and \code{W}/\code{thetaP} were \textit{worse} than the 2-param solver. The textbook signature appeared: \textbf{low total loss at the wrong parameters.}
\end{itemize}

\section*{Phase 5 --- Root-cause proof + the ODE-fit that actually works (June 30)}
\code{PINN-inverse-multicond/}. This is the pivotal diagnostic session. Proved with controlled experiments \textbf{why} the PINN tops out:
\begin{itemize}
\item \code{W} freezes within 100 epochs while loss keeps falling 100$\times$ $\rightarrow$ \textbf{gradient starvation} (the huge state net drives the residual to zero at wrong $\theta$, starving the $\theta$-gradient).
\item Denser/cleaner data didn't fix it. Gradient-matching floored at \textbf{autodiff-derivative noise} (lower residual gave a \textit{worse} \code{W}=0.50).
\item \textbf{Conclusion: the PINN has its own ceiling $\sim$8/36}, from net-derivative bias + gradient starvation, on top of the fundamental ill-posedness.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{The fix that worked --- drop the neural net entirely:} classical \textbf{multiple-condition ODE-fit} (\code{recover\_odefit.py}, scipy \code{least\_squares} trust-region over 6 conditions). No net-derivative noise $\rightarrow$ \textbf{18/17/13/13 under 10\%} (Normal$\rightarrow$Strong), $\sim$2$\times$ the PINN. \code{W}/\code{thetaP} now excellent in low-WNT, still non-identifiable in high-WNT. Established the realistic ceiling: \textbf{$\sim$13--18/36 by any method; only more \textit{information} lifts it.}

\section*{Phase 6 --- Two failed/successful attacks on the levers (July 1--2)}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{\code{-multicond-better} (July 1): architecture lever --- REJECTED.} Smaller weight-decayed nets + alternating two-timescale optimization, hoping to raise the \textit{PINN} count. Result \textbf{9/10/2/4} --- flat-to-worse. \textbf{Net architecture is not the lever.}
\item \textbf{\code{-multicond-excite} (July 1): information lever --- CONFIRMED.} Added 4 conditions that directly perturb the WNT \& MYC nodes (exogenous pulses) to ``excite the dark half of the network.'' ODE-fit rose to \textbf{24/23/21/14} vs 18/17/13/13 baseline (+6/+6/+8/+1). Information works; Strong APC-mutant still stuck (the \code{thetaP} wall).
\item \textbf{\code{pinn-boost} (July 2): break the PINN's \textit{derivative} ceiling --- CONFIRMED.} The key idea: swap the biased autodiff \code{dz/dt} for a \textbf{derivative-free trapezoidal/integral residual} (multiple-shooting), plus relative-error weighting, deterministic collocation, multi-start, and a SIREN option. Result: the \textbf{PINN itself} jumped to \textbf{17/16/10/7} vs the 10/4/5/7 baseline (+24 total, $\sim$2$\times$). The integral residual breaks the autodiff half of the PINN ceiling. Strong APC-mutant unmoved --- structural, not derivative-limited.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Phase 7 --- Rigorous identifiability verdicts (July 5--6)}
Having a point estimate + ad-hoc verdict wasn't enough. Ran three independent identifiability analyses, all pointing at the same wall:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Profile-likelihood} (Raue-style, \code{identifiability} pkg) across the excite/multicond/pinn-boost folders.
\item \textbf{Fisher Information Matrix} (\code{PINN-fisher-matrix/}): each regime has exactly 1 hard-null direction, whose dominant param marches \code{deltaP1} $\rightarrow$ \code{thetaP}+\code{deltaP1} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{\code{thetaP}} as WNT rises; $|R|>0.99$ correlated pairs climb 0$\rightarrow$2$\rightarrow$7$\rightarrow$10 with WNT drive. cond(FIM) 1e7--1e17 (severely sloppy).
\item \textbf{Reduced 8-param FIM} (\code{-top8/}): restricting to the 8 most-identifiable params gives a clean well-posed contrast --- cond 1e2--1e3, 0 null directions, all 8 IDENT. Proves the sloppiness is about \textit{which} params, not the method.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Phase 8 --- The Bayesian / UQ turn (July 7--8, ongoing)}
Move from point estimates to \textbf{posteriors}. SciML Project 1.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{\code{PINN-bayesian} (inverse):} HMC over the 36 params with the pinn-boost integral-residual nets \textbf{frozen} --- the Bayesian version of Stage-3 refine. Deliverable: per-param posterior marginals; a marginal reverting to prior = honest non-identifiability. The smoke test auto-discovered the \code{deltaP1}--\code{thetaP} degeneracy (corr 0.998).
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{$\triangle$ First full run (July 7) is BROKEN --- do not trust the ``36/36 IDENT'' headline.} Miscalibration: the physics likelihood summed the residual over $\sim$210k collocation terms with tiny $\sigma$ $\rightarrow$ absurdly sharp, biased posterior; ESS$\approx$3, truth 30--50$\sigma$ out. Honest recovery is only 19/20/11/8. \textbf{Being reran (\code{pinn\_bayes\_fix})} with a grid-invariant likelihood (mean residual $\times$ fixed effective N) + an ESS/coverage honesty gate.
\end{itemize}
\item \textbf{\code{PINN-forward-bayesian} (forward twin):} params known, HMC over the network \textbf{weights} $\rightarrow$ posterior-predictive trajectory band (UQ on the forward solve). First runs under-covered (0.73$\rightarrow$0.56); relaunched with more warmup/draws/leapfrog.
\end{itemize}

\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\section*{The one-sentence throughline}
\textbf{Forward PINN works once you fix spectral bias (Fourier features) and stop cheating with dense labels; the inverse problem is fundamentally ill-posed ($\sim$13--18/36 ceiling), the PINN adds its own lower ceiling ($\sim$8/36) from autodiff-derivative bias + gradient starvation, the \textit{integral residual} breaks the derivative half and \textit{more exciting conditions} is the only lever that raises the fundamental ceiling --- and no method moves the high-WNT \code{thetaP} wall, which three independent identifiability analyses (profile-likelihood, FIM, and now Bayesian HMC) confirm is a real structural/sloppiness limit, not a tuning failure.}
