\opening{Dear Editors,}

We submit the manuscript \emph{``Estimator or experiment? A Cram\'er--Rao
accounting of parameter recovery by physics-informed and hybrid
neural--mechanistic models of WNT--retinoic acid--HOX signalling in colorectal
cancer''} for consideration as a Research Article.

Physics-informed neural networks and neural--mechanistic hybrid models are
increasingly used to estimate parameters of signalling models, and their
failures are attributed either to the estimator or to non-identifiability, two
diagnoses that call for opposite remedies and are rarely told apart. Our
manuscript shows that the Cram\'er--Rao bound of the experiment actually
performed separates them, and carries this accounting through one model, a
seven-state description of WNT, retinoic acid and HOX signalling across four
stages of APC loss, at three levels of difficulty: the inverse PINN, a new
Schur-complement Gauss--Newton PINN that closes the estimator gap and reports
its own precision, and hybrid models in which a learned regulatory term creates
an information gap that only a designed knockdown removes.

The biological insight is that the parameter defining the disease stage, APC
functionality, is the one a conventional retinoid/WNT perturbation design
informs least in the high-WNT states that matter most, because
$\beta$-catenin saturates its readouts, and that a graded WNT knockdown restores
it. For hybrid models, we show that the basal transcription rate beside a
learned regulation is identifiable only under conditions that silence the
regulator (or with a known partial knockdown of the basal term), give a
training-free criterion and Fisher analysis for designing such conditions, and
test the resulting design rule prospectively, reporting its failed predictions
alongside its successes.

All data are synthetic, so that every claim can be checked against the truth;
all code, run outputs and figure-audit files will be public at submission. The
manuscript has not been published or submitted elsewhere, and both authors
approve the submission.
