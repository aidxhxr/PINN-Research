## Per-triad detail

**1. Mussel bed — the only fully parameterised engineer term.** van de Koppel et al. 2005 *Am Nat* (doi:10.1086/428362), extracted from the KNAW PDF. The mussel equation is ∂M/∂t = ecAM − d_M·M·k_M/(k_M+M) + D∇²M — per-capita mortality falls hyperbolically with *own* density, which is the K_u(u) = K1 + βu/(1+γu) form in loss-rate form. **Table 1 verbatim:** A_up = 1 g/m³ (Cadée & Hegeman 2002); h = 0.1 m (Wildish & Kristmanson 1984; Muschenheim & Newell 1992); f = 100 m³/m³/h (estimated); c = 0.1 m³/g/h (Scholten & Smaal 1998; Riisgård 2001); e = 0.2 g/g (Sukhotin et al. 2002); d_M = 0.02 g/g/h (estimated); **k_M = 150 g/m² (estimated)**; V = 360 m/h ≡ 10 cm/s (Brinkman et al. 2002); **D = 0.0005 m²/h (estimated)**. Measured band wavelength ≈ 6 m. Caveat: the saturation constant (γ) and the diffusivity are the *estimated* ones. Supporting: de Paoli 2017 *PNAS* (PMC5544259) densities 2.7 / 5.6 / 2.5 kg m⁻², bands 5–10 m, isolated mussels move 26–39% slower, cover loss 4.98 ± 1.68 vs 0.87 ± 0.19 %/day patterned. Liu 2012 *Proc R Soc B* (doi:10.1098/rspb.2012.0157) sediment-accumulation variant with saturating uptake. Sherratt 2021 (PMC8384834) and Bennett & Sherratt 2019 (PMC6510835) nondimensional RD analyses (α = 0.6667, δ = 0.15, μ = 0.1333, ν = 100–300) confirm the m/(1+ξm) saturating form. **Gap: the barnacle/macroalgae competitor pair is unparameterised and not shown to be founder-controlled.**

**2. Seagrass (van der Heide 2012, PMC3414520 / doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0042060).** dZ/dt = rZ(1−Z/K)f_S(t) − mZ − G·Z/(h₁+Z) + d_Z∇²Z; dH/dt = s·Z/(h₂+Z) − eH + d_H∇²H. The only paper found where the engineer's own-density benefit is written in the exact saturating form, with **both** diffusivities present and a bifurcation showing bistability. Note the structure: engineering acts through a separate lagged variable H (sediment height) with its own decay, driven by a saturating function of Z — exactly the "legacy variable S" of refinement step 1. Verified values: K = 5976 shoots m⁻², s = 0.00035 m day⁻¹, G_max = 0.03 ind m⁻², giving-up density 0.1K ≈ 598 shoots m⁻², hummocks 5.8 ± 1.2 cm above hollows. **UNVERIFIED:** r, m, e, h₁, h₂, d_Z, d_H — Table 1 is an image.

**3. Coral.** Best empirical founder-control evidence: Schmitt et al. 2019 *PNAS* (PMC6410839) — hysteresis test, macroalgal outcome depended on initial state (fore reef P = 0.008, lagoon P = 0.016); 88% of high-disturbance replicates stayed turf, 88% of moderate returned to *Turbinaria*. Adam et al. 2022 *Ecology* (PMC10078572): "whichever colonizes first can suppress the competitor" (macroalgae ~25% cover when coral <5%; recruitment down five-fold under *Lobophora*). Models with full parameter tables: Briggs 2018 (PMC6143192), Sura 2025 (doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013221) which uses **Holling-II herbivory — the same a_ij N/(1+h_ij N) form**, Fung 2011 (doi:10.1890/10-0378.1), Mumby 2007, Blackwood 2010/2011. Problem: the engineer's self-facilitation is a size refuge, not a density-dependent K, and turf/macroalgae are a maturation chain, not a saddle.

**4. Beaver/elk/moose.** Wolf, Cooper & Hobbs 2007 *Ecol Appl* (doi:10.1890/06-2042.1, read in full) is an explicit two-state ASS paper: stable beaver–willow state vs alternative elk–grassland state, channel incision as the switch. Marshall et al. 2013 *Proc R Soc B* (PMC3574379): after 10 yr, control 117 cm, exclosed 160 cm, dammed+browsed 174 cm, dammed+unbrowsed 248 cm; dams raised water tables 33 ± 6 cm; dammed plots 90% more biomass; 2 m is the recovery threshold. Baker 2012 *Ecosphere* (doi:10.1890/ES12-00058.1): beaver persist indefinitely at ≤20 elk km⁻², persistence declines exponentially from 30 to 60 elk km⁻². But SAVANNA simulation, not a 3-ODE fit, and the willow benefit is shared.

**5. *V. cholerae*.** Nadell & Bassler 2011 *PNAS* (PMC3161532): EPS⁺ μ_max = 0.139 h⁻¹ vs EPS⁻ 0.183 h⁻¹ (25% cost); selection coefficient +0.144 h⁻¹ in biofilm and −0.044 h⁻¹ in liquid. Nadell 2015 *ISME J* (PMC4511925): RbmA benefit is private, RbmC shared; immotile invaders "including cells genetically identical to the resident strain" show almost no colonisation — a clean priority effect. Yan 2017 *Nat Commun* (PMC5569112) osmotic cheater exclusion. **No three-strain system, no saturating benefit-vs-density curve, no diffusivity.**

**6. Spartina/Salicornia/Scirpus.** Bouma 2009 *Oikos* is the right paper conceptually — local accretion vs surrounding erosion troughs, both density-dependent with thresholds — but closed access and no numeric threshold density retrievable (UNVERIFIED). Same for van Hulzen 2007 and Silinski 2016 (doi:10.1016/j.geomorph.2016.09.006). Schwarz 2018 *Nat Geosci* paywalled everywhere. Competition data only as RNE indices: Li et al. 2011 (PMC3187781) shows *S. anglica* × *S. triqueter* RNE positive for Spartina at all N, but **flips to facilitation for Scirpus at high N** — the pair is not a stable founder-control block.

## Recommendation

**Name the intertidal mussel bed (*Mytilus edulis*), and cite van de Koppel et al. 2005 Table 1 as the parameter source.** It is the only system where the published engineer term has the model's functional shape and a single sourced table. Quote: mortality term d_M·k_M/(k_M + M) with k_M = 150 g m⁻², d_M = 0.02 g g⁻¹ h⁻¹, D = 5×10⁻⁴ m² h⁻¹, V = 10 cm s⁻¹, ~6 m band wavelength; add de Paoli 2017's 2.5–5× resilience contrast as the patch-expand-vs-collapse (Maxwell) evidence.

**Second choice for the K_u(u) form specifically:** van der Heide 2012's seagrass model — the literal βu/(1+γu) with two diffusivities and published bistability.

**For the v–w founder-control sub-block, import it** — no engineer system has it. Defensible citation: Schmitt et al. 2019 *PNAS* plus Adam et al. 2022 *Ecology*.

**For the priority-effect/critical-patch prediction, quote Michaels et al. 2020 *Ecology*** (doi:10.1002/ecy.3099), which formalises the Maxwell point as a critical patch radius with measured values: *Sphagnum* transplants at r = 0.035 m decline while r = 0.07 m grow; *Spartina alterniflora* patches <1 m² degrade under drought while >20 m² persist (Angelini 2016, PMC4992128 — cordgrass survival 64.3 ± 27.4% with mussels vs 1.0 ± 0.06% without).

**UNVERIFIED:** Bouma 2009 threshold densities; van Hulzen 2007 accretion-vs-density; Silinski 2016 expansion rates; Schwarz 2018 traits and model; van der Heide 2012 r, m, e, h₁, h₂, d_Z, d_H; Fung 2011 and Mumby 2007 parameter tables; Liu 2012 numeric table; Baker 2005.
